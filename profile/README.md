# Welcome to Meridian

We are a collaborative team of engineers and developers driven by curiosity, creativity, and a passion for building things.

Our work spans both **software and hardware**, bringing together code and electronics to turn ideas into practical solutions. From developing software applications and tools to designing electronic systems and experimenting, we enjoy 
exploring the possibilities of what we can create.

## What We Do

- **Software Development** — Building applications, tools, and systems through thoughtful design and development.
- **Hardware & Electronics** — Designing, prototyping, and experimenting with electronic circuits and embedded systems.

## Our Approach

We believe the best projects come from collaboration, experimentation, and a willingness to learn. Every project is an opportunity to develop new skills, explore unfamiliar technologies, and build something meaningful together.
We value the input from potential users of our tools and designs and are open for discussions on improvements or features.

This organization is where we share our work, document our progress, and bring our ideas to life.

Check out our current project, an electronic component part tracker: [PartBuddy](https://github.com/Meridian-devs/PartBuddy)

**- Meridian Team**
